Drawdown-Guard

An investor can say how much they are willing to lose. A portfolio cannot keep that promise on its own. Every weekday this agent measures the client’s book against the loss they agreed to, and when the promise stops holding it buys back the difference — the cheapest structure that floors the loss at every depth, never a view on where the market is going.
