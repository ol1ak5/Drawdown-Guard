Drawdown-Guard

An investor can say how much they are willing to lose. A portfolio cannot keep that promise on its own. Every weekday this agent measures the client’s book against the loss they agreed to, and when the promise stops holding it buys back the difference — the cheapest structure that floors the loss at every depth, never a view on where the market is going.

What changed this morning

The list is arithmetic — the book against yesterday’s snapshot. Below it, a language model is handed the holdings, the promise, the protection held and the four stress rungs, and asked which positions carry a risk issue. It is not given the answer; finding it is the question. Nothing it says reaches an order — the hedge is sized from the ladder below, and a finding the cycle did not act on is recorded as a disagreement rather than resolved.
