Drawdown-Guard

An investor can say how much they are willing to lose. A portfolio cannot keep that promise on its own. Every weekday this agent measures the client’s book against the loss they agreed to, and when the promise stops holding it buys back the difference — the cheapest structure that floors the loss at every depth, never a view on where the market is going.

Your mandate sets a downside budget of 100,589 against your 604,459 in equity exposure, meaning a market drop would have cost 503,870 more than you agreed to risk. To eliminate that shortfall, we bought four SPY 670 puts, three QQQ 650 puts, and six IWM 270 puts, which ensures your losses stop below those strikes. That cost 25,978 in cash with zero upside given up. Collars were not used because selling calls to fund the hedge would have given away upside priced well below the puts.

The floor holds at every depth

Four rungs are measured every cycle and the line between them is straight — the payoff bends only at a strike, so this is the exact answer between the points, not a curve fitted to them. Drag it. Nothing here predicts a fall; it answers what this book would be worth if one happened.
